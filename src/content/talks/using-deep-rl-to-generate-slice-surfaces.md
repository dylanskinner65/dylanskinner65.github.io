The first version of this work, and a longer treatment than the talk I gave a year later — a hundred slides, most of them building up the background.

A fancy way of saying I used deep learning to try to find a specific invariant of a knot. The talk starts from what reinforcement learning is, works through knots and braid notation, and arrives at the problem of generating slice surfaces by searching over braid transformations.
