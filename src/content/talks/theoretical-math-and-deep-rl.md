The same knot theory project, compressed into a lightning talk and pointed at a different audience — people who want the reinforcement learning and the engineering, not the algebraic topology.

Which turns out to be a useful exercise: pure math problems make unusually clean RL environments. The action space is small and discrete, the reward is well-defined, and there's no simulator gap to worry about because the environment *is* the mathematics.
